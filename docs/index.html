Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

MacroSnap ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Photos & Images

When you use the food scanning or barcode features, we capture photos of your food. These images are sent to Google Gemini AI for analysis and are not stored on our servers after processing.

Account Information

You can sign in with Google or your phone number. We collect your email address or phone number (depending on how you sign in) and the display name you choose, used only to identify your account via Firebase Authentication.

Meal, Habit & Diet Data

Your logged meals, nutrition targets, habit trackers, water logs and diet preferences are stored on your device and saved to the cloud automatically whenever you are signed in. There is nothing you need to press — every change syncs in the background so your data survives reinstalls and device changes.

AI Assistant Conversations

Messages you send to Guru, the in-app nutrition assistant (which answers food, nutrition, protein and your own progress questions), are sent to our backend and forwarded to Google Gemini AI to generate a reply. Along with the last few messages, Guru receives a summary of your own logged data — today's meals and macro totals, your nutrition targets, habit progress, water intake and the display name you chose — so it can answer questions about your day. Your phone number and email are never included; this data is not sold or shared for advertising.

Subscription Information

Subscriptions are handled by Razorpay. We store only your subscription status and start date — no card, UPI or banking details are ever stored on our servers. New accounts also receive a one-time 30-day free plan; we record only the start date of that trial.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered food recognition and nutrition analysis
  • To generate personalised diet plans and power Guru, the nutrition assistant
  • To maintain your meal history, habit streaks and progress
  • To restore your data when you sign in on a new device
  • To manage subscriptions and the one-time 30-day free plan
  • To send local meal and habit reminder notifications you enable

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the following ways:

  • Local Storage: A copy of your meals, habits, preferences and settings is kept on your device so the app works offline
  • Cloud Sync: When you sign in, your data is saved automatically and continuously to Cloud Firestore (Firebase), keyed to your account, so it restores on any device you sign into
  • Firebase: Authentication is handled by Firebase (Google)
  • Photos & scans: Food images are sent to Google Gemini AI for analysis and are not retained

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase – Authentication and cloud data storage
  • Google Gemini AI – Food recognition, diet plans and Guru, the nutrition assistant
  • Razorpay – Subscription payments (UPI, cards and other supported methods)

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and secure storage practices. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data — use the in-app Delete Account option in Settings, or see the account deletion page
  • Opt out of data collection by uninstalling the app
  • Request deletion of cloud-synced data by contacting us

7. Children's Privacy

MacroSnap is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
