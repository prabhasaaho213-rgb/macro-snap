Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

MacroSnap ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Photos & Images

A photo you take or pick while logging a meal is sent through our backend to Google Gemini AI for analysis — we keep the analysis result, not the photo. A copy of a logged meal's photo is then kept on your device only, for the window you choose in Settings → Meal Photos: 24 hours (the default), 7 days, 30 days, or Forever (Pro).

Optional cloud photo backup (Pro, off by default): if you turn on Settings → Sync Meal Photos, photos of meals you log are also stored in Cloud Firestore so they can reappear on a new phone. Turn it off at any time; deleting a meal deletes its cloud photo, and deleting your account deletes all of them. We never use your photos for advertising or model training.

Account Information

You sign in with Google — there is no guest mode. We collect the email address of the Google account you sign in with and the display name you choose, used only to identify your account via Firebase Authentication. Your account is identified by a Firebase user ID; a verified phone number is also accepted as an account identifier for accounts created that way.

Meal, Habit & Diet Data

Your logged meals, nutrition targets, habit trackers, water logs and diet preferences are stored on your device and backed up to the cloud automatically whenever you are signed in. There is nothing you need to press — every change is backed up in the background so your data survives reinstalls and device changes.

The cloud copy is a backup, not a live two-way merge: meals you add or delete reach your other devices, but an edit made to an existing meal on one device is not merged into the copy on another, and habits and the water log are stored as one document per account, so the most recent save wins.

Leaderboard (optional)

If you join the Weekly Leaderboard, your chosen display name, your area (state and district) and your weekly points are visible to other signed-in MacroSnap users in the same area. Your meals, weight, photos and habits are never shown there. Weekly points are derived from activity you already log — meals, habits, water, weigh-ins and your step totals — and are stored per week.

Health & fitness data (optional)

Only if you grant the permission, the app reads step counts and active-calorie totals from Health Connect on Android (or Apple Health on iOS) to show your movement and count it towards habits and leaderboard points. The raw health records stay on your device; only per-day step totals are uploaded to your account so a reinstall can restore them. You can revoke the permission at any time in the system settings, and the app keeps working without it. The phone's own step sensor is also used and needs no health permission.

AI Assistant Conversations

Messages you send to Guru, the in-app nutrition assistant (which answers food, nutrition, protein and your own progress questions), are sent to our backend and forwarded to Google Gemini AI to generate a reply. Along with the last few messages, Guru receives a summary of your own logged data — today's meals and macro totals, your nutrition targets, habit progress, water intake and the display name you chose — so it can answer questions about your day. Your phone number and email are never included; this data is not sold or shared for advertising.

Subscription Information

Subscriptions are handled by Razorpay. We store only your subscription status and start date — no card, UPI or banking details are ever stored on our servers. Pro costs ₹49/month; a new account's first month is ₹1, charged up front, and you approve the monthly ₹49 autopay mandate at checkout — we record only your subscription status and start date.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered food recognition and nutrition analysis
  • To generate personalised diet plans and power Guru, the nutrition assistant
  • To maintain your meal history, habit streaks and progress
  • To restore your data when you sign in on a new device
  • To manage subscriptions, the one-time ₹1 first month and monthly renewals
  • To send local meal and habit reminder notifications you enable

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the following ways:

  • Local Storage: A copy of your meals, habits, preferences and settings is kept on your device so the app works offline
  • Cloud Sync: When you sign in, your data is saved automatically and continuously to Cloud Firestore (Firebase), keyed to your account, so it restores on any device you sign into
  • Firebase: Authentication is handled by Firebase (Google)
  • Photos & scans: Food images are sent to Google Gemini AI for analysis. We store no copy of the image on our servers — it is decoded in memory, sent, and discarded. Google's own terms govern what happens at its end: images and prompts sent through the Gemini API are logged for a limited period for abuse monitoring (currently up to 55 days), and when the API is used on a billed project — which is how MacroSnap runs it — Google does not use that content to improve its products. If the key were ever moved to the free (unbilled) tier, Google's terms would allow it to use that content to improve its services and to have reviewers read it, which is why production stays on the billed tier. A copy of a logged meal's photo is kept on your device for the window you choose, and optionally in Cloud Firestore if you enable Pro photo backup
  • Deletion record: when you delete your account, we keep exactly one record: a one-way, keyed fingerprint (an HMAC) of the email address you signed up with, and the deletion date. It cannot be turned back into your email address without our server key, it contains no meals, habits, name, phone, photos, history or trial data, it is not linked to any active account, and the app cannot read it. Its only purpose is to stop the once-per-account ₹1 first-month offer being claimed again by deleting and re-registering. It stops counting after 24 months, and the record itself is deleted at the next account deletion (there is no daily cleanup job — the purge runs when an account is deleted). You can ask us to erase it sooner at macrosnap7@gmail.com — the only consequence is that the ₹1 offer could then be claimed again with that address

  • Razorpay – Subscription payments (UPI, cards and other supported methods)

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and secure storage practices. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data — use the in-app Delete Account option in Settings, or see the account deletion page. The identifier-only deletion record described in section 3 is the one thing kept
  • Opt out of data collection by uninstalling the app
  • Request deletion of cloud-synced data by contacting us
  • Request erasure of the deletion fingerprint described in section 3, or a copy of your data, by emailing macrosnap7@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, and erasing the fingerprint does not require an account — the request itself is the only record we keep of it
